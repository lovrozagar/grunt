rulesync canonical event is `beforeSubmitPrompt` (→ Claude/Codex `UserPromptSubmit`), not `userPromptSubmit` — an unknown key makes Zod reject the whole `.rulesync/hooks.jsonc`, emitting nothing while `--check` still reports up to date.
